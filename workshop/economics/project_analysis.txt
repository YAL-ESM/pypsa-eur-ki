PROJECT ANALYSIS - Brazilian-German HVDC interconnector
Role 4 (economic analysis) - consolidated information
Status: 2025-11-27
================================================================================

This file collects everything the group has for the project analysis: the
decided study design, what each role has delivered, the consolidated costs and
the status. It is the single reference for the final results.


1. STUDY DESIGN (DECIDED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Study type:  cost-benefit analysis of a given cable. No capacity expansion;
               the cable size is fixed, the model only dispatches it.
- Base year:   2025 (present-value reference year).
- Currency:    EUR, real 2025 prices, everywhere (Germany, Brazil, cable).
- Landing:     German node 08 (bus DE0 8AC), North.
- Construction: 6-7 years for the cable.
- Comparison:  system with the cable against the system without it.
- Discount rate: one study rate, 2% real (band 1-3%), applied in POST-PROCESSING
               to the net present value. The model's own discount rate (7% for
               asset annualisation) is role 3's and is not changed here.
- Role 4 works from the model outputs in post-processing; it does not change
   the model's discount rates.

3. BRAZIL - ROLE 2 (workshop/brazil)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Brazil is a dedicated export site: no Brazilian demand, no Brazilian grid.

Landing point: north coast of Rio Grande do Norte (RN), Macau / Guamare
  Latitude -5.0667, longitude -36.4517.

Firm supply, variant A (with connections, onshore-wind mix): enough to deliver
2 GW into the cable in every hour of weather year 2013.
  Solar PV       8.68 GW   (capacity factor ~0.19)
  Onshore wind   3.47 GW   (capacity factor ~0.50)
  Battery        3.20 GW / 30 GWh
  Connection to the landing: 38 M EUR/yr.

Hourly time series (aggregate output per technology, 8,760 h, weather year 2013):
  workshop/brazil/timeseries/firm_supply_timeseries.csv        variant A (used)
  workshop/brazil/timeseries/firm_supply_timeseries_noconn.csv variant B (offshore)

Asset costs, real 2025 EUR, annualised at 7% (converted from role 2's 2035
figures using the model's 2025 cost data):
  Solar PV              58,293 EUR/MW/yr   (58.3 EUR/kW/yr)
  Onshore wind         135,624 EUR/MW/yr   (135.6 EUR/kW/yr)
  Battery storage       22,387 EUR/MWh/yr  (22.4 EUR/kWh/yr)
  Battery inverter      41,651 EUR/MW/yr   (41.7 EUR/kW/yr)

Caveat: these are European cost data used as proxies; Brazilian costs are not
derived yet. The connection cost is still on role 2's 2035 basis (small item).


4. GERMAN SYSTEM - ROLE 3
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Base run: trade variant "de10-trade" - Germany with six neighbours (FR, NL, PL,
DK, AT, CH), 17 nodes, 3-hour steps, cross-border capacity s_max_pu 0.3, CO2
price and cap shared with Germany. This is the reference system the cable is
judged against. Role 3 is providing the solved networks with and without the
project (the runs are too large for this workspace).

2050: the zero-CO2 cap is relaxed, but 2050 stays as a target year. This removes
the artefact role 3 flagged (in the strict baseline the cap makes clean power
look enormously valuable in 2050 alone).

Known effect (role 3 caveat, workshop/trade/caveat-2050.md): the cable is very
expensive per MWh delivered, so it does not pay against 2025-2045 German prices.


5. ECONOMICS AND WIRING - ROLE 4
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Wiring (adds Brazil and the cable to the model at network composition):
  scripts/add_brazil_cable.py     the components
  scripts/compose_network.py      calls it (behind the config flag)
  config/config.brazil-cable.yaml route, landing, capacities, consolidated costs

Components added:
  - Brazilian AC bus and battery bus at the landing;
  - solar and onshore-wind generators at the fixed variant-A capacities, with
    role 2's hourly profiles as p_max_pu;
  - battery as Store + charger + discharger (3,200 MW / 30 GWh);
  - cable Link BR0 0AC -> DE0 8AC, 2 GW fixed, efficiency from the route's
    full-load losses, availability from the fault days, lifetime 40, capital
    cost from role 1's aggregates, carrier "Brazil cable".

How to run the case with the project (once the base inputs exist):
  pixi run snakemake results/de10-brazil/networks/solved_2050.nc -c4 \
    --configfile config/config.workshop.yaml config/config.trade.yaml \
    config/config.brazil-cable.yaml --forcerun compose_network

Post-processing (role 4): net present value at 2% real (1-3% band), system cost
with vs without, cable utilisation, prices, emissions, who gains and who loses.
The results template is workshop/economics/results_deck_template.pdf.

7. OPEN POINTS / CAVEATS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Brazilian generation costs are European proxies, not derived Brazilian costs.
- The connection cost (38 M EUR/yr) is still on role 2's 2035 basis.
- Cable losses are modelled as a single full-load efficiency; availability as a
  constant from the average fault days.
- The 2050 horizon dominates the apparent value; results are reported across the
  1-3% discount band and with 2050 flagged.
