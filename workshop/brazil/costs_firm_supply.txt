Costs of a steady 2 GW into the cable, north coast of Rio Grande do Norte (RN), Brazil
======================================================================================
Source: workshop/brazil/firm_supply.py. Weather year 2013 (ERA5). Plants within 150 km of the landing,
battery at the landing, at least 2 GW into the cable in every hour.
Costs: PyPSA-Eur European cost data for 2035 (annualised investment + fixed O&M, 7% discount rate).
Brazilian costs are not derived yet: read the figures as a comparison, not as Brazilian prices.

Cost assumptions (annualised)
  Solar PV                      44.3 EUR/kW/yr   (investment 443 EUR/kW, lifetime 40 yr)
  Onshore wind                 124.5 EUR/kW/yr   (investment 1,345 EUR/kW, lifetime 30 yr)
  Offshore wind, fixed         210.3 EUR/kW/yr   (investment 2,040 EUR/kW, lifetime 30 yr)
  Offshore wind, floating      287.9 EUR/kW/yr   (investment 2,710 EUR/kW, lifetime 20 yr)
  Battery storage               13.1 EUR/kWh/yr  (investment 158 EUR/kWh)
  Battery inverter              25.5 EUR/kW/yr   (investment 174 EUR/kW, efficiency 0.96)
  Connection on land            64.8 EUR/MW/km/yr (HVAC overhead line)
  Connection at sea            432.0 EUR/MW/km/yr (AC submarine cable) + substation 73.8 EUR/kW/yr
